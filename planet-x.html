Sector wheel

Each sector holds exactly one object · Visible sky 1–6
Move
Pick a pawn, then use an action button or tap a sector on the pawn track (the light ring just inside the numbers). Tapping a sector a little behind a pawn moves it back to fix a slip. The pawn farthest back plays next, and the earth board turns to follow it, stopping for theory phases and the Planet X conference.
